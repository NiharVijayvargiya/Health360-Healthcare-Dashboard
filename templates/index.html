About the project

The problem

Chronic conditions such as heart disease, diabetes and stroke are often found late. Going through patient records by hand makes early patterns hard to see.

The data

A smart healthcare dataset of 5,000 patients: age, gender, BMI, exercise level, smoking, alcohol, blood pressure, cholesterol, glucose, symptoms and disease flags.

The tools

Tableau Desktop for analysis, Tableau Public for hosting, and Flask to embed the dashboard and story in this page.
